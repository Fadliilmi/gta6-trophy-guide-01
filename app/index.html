Roadmap to the platinum ~200 hours · difficulty 4/10 · 1 playthrough

Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap and GTABase trophy guide. All 55 trophies are single-player — there are no online trophies. There are no difficulty settings and no mission replay, but free-roam continues after the story.

Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

Five chapters, 12 story trophies, all automatic and unmissable. Along the way: do optional missions as they appear (100% needs any 100 of them), scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app (100% needs 200 of 277 base models) and study new animals through the binoculars (100% needs 100 of 255). There are no mission medals, so nothing needs replaying for a rating.

Step 2 — 100% completion checklist ~100 hours

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion. You need 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 15 of 18 meditation spots; robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s Target Decks need everything. Progress made during the story already counts, and nothing on the list is missable.

Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

Strangers & Freaks strands are not part of 100%, so finish the 8 that count, then sweep up whatever is left on the checklist — the platinum follows. The hardest single trophy is A Long Road (100% completion).

⚠ The one missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning and walks to the balcony at Jason’s house. Follow her and accept — that meditation is gone once Chapter 1 ends, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it to be safe, and keep a manual save from the start of each chapter: the game has 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave, and no mission replay. PowerPyx · Output Lag

Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your platinum save. (PowerPyx, GTABase.)
